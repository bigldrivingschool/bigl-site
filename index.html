What we offer

From your first lesson to your full licence test, we're here for every step of the journey.

Learner Licence

We can help you prepare for the theory test on the NZ Road Codes with access to the right resources.

Restricted Licence

In-depth knowledge of Driver Testing and restricted test routes in the Hibiscus Coast area.

Full Licence

Prepare for your Full Licence Test with manoeuvres up to 70 km/h and hazard identification training.

Overseas Conversion

Assistance with licence conversion, NZ Road Codes, theory test, and the Full Driving Licence test.

Mock Test

Practice on real test routes with a full 1-hour mock test for Restricted or Full licence.
