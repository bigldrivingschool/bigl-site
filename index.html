Frequently asked questions

Common questions about lessons, pricing and tests.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Millwater, Silverdale and provide driving lessons across the Hibiscus Coast and surrounding areas, including Orewa, Red Beach, Stanmore Bay, Whangaparāoa, Manly, Gulf Harbour and Stillwater.

How much do driving lessons cost?

Lessons are $80 for a full 1 hour of driving, $460 for 6 x 1 hour lessons, or $750 for 10 x 1 hour lessons. A 1 hour mock test is $85. Add $5 per hour to use the driving school vehicle. A 1 hour lesson includes take-home lesson notes.

Who will my instructor be?

Pravin Kalyan (PK) is NZTA qualified and holds the dual qualification of Driving Instructor and Driver Testing Officer. He is an ex-VTNZ Driver Testing Officer, has conducted thousands of restricted and full driving tests, and has 37 years of driving experience.

Do you offer mock tests?

Yes. A mock test is a full 1 hour of driving on real test routes, for either the Restricted or Full licence test. It is recommended before taking your actual driving test, or before rebooking if you have previously failed and lost confidence.

Can I use the driving school vehicle for my lesson?

Yes. Lessons can be taken in the driving school vehicle for an additional $5 per hour.

Do you help with overseas licence conversion?

Yes. We help you become familiar with New Zealand driving conditions and requirements, and prepare you for the overseas conversion driving test, which is based on the Full Licence Test. We recommend at least 6 to 8 weeks of driving practice with your supervisor before booking your full driving test.

How much driving practice do I need before my restricted test?

NZTA recommends 120 hours of driving practice before sitting your Restricted Licence test. This can be a combination of driving school lessons and driving with other supervisors between your formal lessons.
