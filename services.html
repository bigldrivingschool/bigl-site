Our services

Everything you need to get your driver licence

Learner Licence

Big L Driving School can help you prepare for the theory test on the NZ Road Codes. We can direct you to available resources and guide you through the process of getting your learner licence. The learner licence is the first step in New Zealand's three-stage driver licensing system and allows you to drive with a supervisor.

Restricted Licence

We can help you achieve your goal of getting your Restricted Licence. We have an added advantage of in-depth knowledge of Driver Testing and the Restricted Test routes in the Hibiscus Coast area. The restricted licence allows you to drive on your own between 5am and 10pm. Our lessons are tailored to prepare you specifically for the restricted test requirements.

Full Licence

Big L Driving School can help you prepare for your Full Licence Test which involves driving into speed zones up to 70 km/h involving various manoeuvres. We can help you in identifying and responding to driving hazards as you progress through the test. The full licence removes all conditions and allows you to drive at any time.

Overseas Conversion

We can assist you with your licence conversion by offering our services in understanding the New Zealand Road Codes and passing the Theory Test followed by the Full Driving Licence test. Whether you're a new resident or visiting, we'll help you navigate the conversion process smoothly.

Mock Test

We can help you achieve your goal of getting your Restricted or Full Licence with a realistic mock test. We have an added advantage of in-depth knowledge of Driver Testing and the test routes in the area. A mock test lets you experience real test conditions and receive feedback on what to improve before the actual test.
