Pravin Kalyan (PK)

Pravin Kalyan is the founder and lead instructor at Big L Driving School. With years of experience on the Hibiscus Coast, PK has helped hundreds of students pass their driving tests on the first attempt.

What sets PK apart is his dual qualification — he is both an NZTA qualified Driving Instructor and a Driver Testing Officer. This means he knows exactly what testers look for and can prepare you thoroughly for every aspect of the driving test.

PK's approach is patient, encouraging, and tailored to each student's needs. Whether you're a nervous beginner or an experienced driver converting an overseas licence, you'll receive professional, friendly guidance every step of the way.
